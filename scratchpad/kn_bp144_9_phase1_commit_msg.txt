KN-BP144-9 Phase 1: synthetic-paste delivery mechanism for interactive browser-LLM composers

Founder-approved after Phase 0's read-only investigation (browser authority response-parsing
failures, 041 mesh test receipt). Phase 0 proved Gemini and ChatGPT receive byte-identical
prompt TEXT via buildMcAwareQueryText, ruling out the ticket's original "prompt construction"
hypothesis and pointing instead at the DOM-level delivery mechanism (Quill vs ProseMirror).

Change: setComposerText() in webview_preload.ts now tries a synthetic ClipboardEvent('paste',
{clipboardData}) dispatch FIRST for contentEditable composers, self-verifying via a DOM readback
(composerReadbackLooksComplete) before trusting it, and falling through to the pre-existing
document.execCommand('insertText', ...) path -- byte-identical to before this pass -- when the
paste path is unavailable or the readback doesn't confirm the full text landed. Additive only:
ChatGPT's already-working ProseMirror composer and Claude's own hand-written driver cannot
regress, since their existing path is untouched and only consulted as a fallback.

Rationale: modern rich-text editors (Quill, ProseMirror, Slate, Draft.js) register their own
'paste' event handler and parse clipboardData through the editor's own normalization pipeline,
a more portable mechanism than a raw execCommand('insertText') that bypasses editor-specific
paragraph/newline handling entirely.
