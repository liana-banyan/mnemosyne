KN-BP144-9 Phase 2: numeric-gate fallthrough fix for matchFreeTextAnswerToOption

Founder-approved after Phase 0's read-only investigation. Phase 0 found the numeric-first branch
in matchFreeTextAnswerToOption was blocking 10 of 32 receipt-041 parse_failed rows: any incidental
number in an authority's answer text (a ratio like "1:2:4", a date, a population figure) forced an
unconditional early return, even on questions whose OWN options are pure prose with no number at
all -- the token-overlap fallback (strategy 2) never even ran for these, regardless of how strong
its match would have been.

Fix: the numeric short-circuit (strategy 1) now only fires -- and only ever returns an honest null
on a tolerance miss -- when optionsContainNumericValue(options) is true, i.e. this question's own
options are themselves numeric-shaped. When they are (qids 27/53/59 in receipt 041), behavior is
byte-for-byte unchanged. When they are not, but the answer text still contains an incidental
number, this now falls through to token-overlap, gated by a NEW, strictly higher score floor
(PSIONIC_OPTION_MATCH_MIN_SCORE_INCIDENTAL_NUMBER = 0.5, vs the ordinary 0.34) -- added after a
real-compiled-dist false-extraction check found the ordinary floor alone let one incidental-number
row (qid 111, user_google) through at a merely-coincidental 0.43 while the genuine match on the
same question (qid 111, user_claude) scored 0.80. The stricter floor is a uniform, question-
agnostic bar for this specific fallthrough shape, not a fix tuned to qid 111 itself; it excludes
the coincidental case while preserving the genuine one. Neither PSIONIC_OPTION_MATCH_MIN_SCORE nor
PSIONIC_OPTION_MATCH_MIN_MARGIN (used by every other, unaffected call path) is touched.

Also exports matchFreeTextAnswerToOption, extractChosenLetterFromAuthorityText, and
resolveAuthorityLetterMatch (previously module-private) SOLELY so verification scripts can import
and replay them directly from the real compiled dist -- no behavior change, closes a Phase 0-
flagged honesty gap (hand-copied reimplementation risked silent divergence from the real source).

tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.main.json: clean. npm run build:main: clean.
