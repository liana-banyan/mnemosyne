## Facts

**Q: What is the scientific method?**
A: The scientific method is a systematic process for investigation: observe, form hypothesis, design experiment, collect data, analyze results, and draw conclusions. It requires reproducibility, peer review, and falsifiability.

**Q: What is climate change and its main causes?**
A: Climate change refers to long-term shifts in global temperatures and weather patterns. Since the Industrial Revolution, human activities (burning fossil fuels, deforestation) have increased atmospheric CO₂, trapping heat.

**Q: What is the concept of falsifiability in science?**
A: Popper's falsifiability criterion states that a scientific hypothesis must be testable and capable of being proven wrong. Unfalsifiable claims are not scientific. Failed falsification attempts strengthen a theory.

**Q: What is artificial intelligence?**
A: Artificial intelligence is the simulation of human intelligence processes by computer systems, including learning (acquiring information), reasoning (using rules to reach conclusions), and self-correction.

**Q: What is blockchain technology?**
A: Blockchain is a distributed ledger technology where records (blocks) are chained together cryptographically and stored across a network. It enables trustless, transparent, immutable record-keeping without central authority.

**Q: What is the UN and its main purposes?**
A: The United Nations (founded 1945) is an international organization with 193 member states. Its main purposes: maintain international peace and security, develop friendly relations among nations, and promote social progress.

**Q: What is the concept of sustainability?**
A: Sustainability means meeting present needs without compromising future generations' ability to meet theirs (Brundtland definition). Environmental sustainability focuses on resource use, biodiversity, and climate stability.

**Q: What is the difference between correlation and causation?**
A: Correlation is a statistical relationship between two variables. Causation means one variable directly causes changes in another. Correlation does not imply causation; confounding variables can create spurious correlations.

**Q: What is globalization?**
A: Globalization is the increasing interconnection of economies, cultures, and people worldwide through trade, communication, and migration. It has expanded markets and reduced poverty but also increased inequality and cultural homogenization.

**Q: What is the concept of a paradigm shift in science?**
A: Kuhn's paradigm shift occurs when accumulated anomalies cause the scientific community to abandon one framework for another. Examples: Copernican revolution, Darwinian evolution, quantum mechanics.

**Q: What is democracy and its key principles?**
A: Democracy is a system of government where power derives from the people, exercised through free elections, rule of law, protection of individual rights, and peaceful transfer of power. Forms include direct and representative democracy.

**Q: What is the greenhouse effect?**
A: The greenhouse effect occurs when atmospheric gases (CO₂, methane, water vapor) absorb and re-emit infrared radiation from Earth's surface, warming the planet. Human activities have enhanced this natural effect.

**Q: What is the concept of epistemological humility?**
A: Epistemological humility acknowledges the limits of one's knowledge and the possibility of being wrong. It is essential to scientific progress, intellectual honesty, and productive dialogue.

**Q: What is the United States Constitution?**
A: The US Constitution (ratified 1788) is the supreme law of the United States, establishing the framework of federal government, separation of powers among three branches, and protecting individual rights through the Bill of Rights.

**Q: What is the concept of a paradigm in social science?**
A: In social science, a paradigm is a framework of basic assumptions and methods. Positivism seeks objective law-like regularities; interpretivism emphasizes understanding subjective meanings; critical theory aims to critique and change social structures.

**Q: What is the Big Bang theory?**
A: The Big Bang theory is the prevailing cosmological model stating the universe originated approximately 13.8 billion years ago from an extremely hot, dense state and has been expanding ever since.

**Q: What is the concept of cognitive load in learning?**
A: Cognitive load theory (Sweller) states that working memory has limited capacity. Instructional design should minimize extraneous load (unnecessary complexity) and optimize germane load (schema formation) to facilitate learning.

**Q: What is the concept of digital literacy?**
A: Digital literacy is the ability to effectively and critically navigate, evaluate, and create information using digital technologies. It encompasses technical skills, critical thinking about online information, and understanding of digital rights.

**Q: What is the concept of cultural relativism?**
A: Cultural relativism holds that cultural practices should be understood within their own context and not judged by the standards of another culture. It promotes anthropological understanding but raises questions about universal human rights.

**Q: What is the concept of a stateless nation?**
A: A stateless nation is an ethnic or cultural group with a shared identity that does not have its own independent state. Examples include Kurds (across Turkey, Iraq, Iran, Syria) and Tibetans.
