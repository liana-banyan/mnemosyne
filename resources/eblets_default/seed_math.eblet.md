## Facts

**Q: What does the Pythagorean theorem state?**
A: In a right triangle, the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides: a² + b² = c².

**Q: What is the definition of a prime number?**
A: A prime number is a natural number greater than 1 that has no positive divisors other than 1 and itself.

**Q: What is Euler's formula for complex exponentials?**
A: Euler's formula states that e^(iθ) = cos(θ) + i·sin(θ), connecting exponential functions with trigonometry.

**Q: What is the fundamental theorem of calculus?**
A: The fundamental theorem of calculus links differentiation and integration: the derivative of the integral of a function equals the original function, and the definite integral can be computed using antiderivatives.

**Q: What is the quadratic formula?**
A: For ax² + bx + c = 0, the solutions are x = (−b ± √(b²−4ac)) / (2a). The discriminant b²−4ac determines the number of real solutions.

**Q: What is the definition of a derivative?**
A: The derivative of a function f at a point x is the limit of (f(x+h)−f(x))/h as h approaches zero, representing the instantaneous rate of change.

**Q: What is the binomial theorem?**
A: The binomial theorem states that (a+b)^n = Σ C(n,k) a^(n−k) b^k for k from 0 to n, where C(n,k) is the binomial coefficient n!/(k!(n−k)!).

**Q: What is a vector space?**
A: A vector space is a set of objects (vectors) that can be added together and multiplied by scalars, satisfying axioms including closure, associativity, commutativity, and distributivity.

**Q: What is the definition of a limit?**
A: The limit of f(x) as x approaches a equals L means that for every ε > 0, there exists δ > 0 such that if 0 < |x−a| < δ then |f(x)−L| < ε.

**Q: What is Bayes' theorem?**
A: Bayes' theorem states P(A|B) = P(B|A)·P(A) / P(B), relating conditional probabilities. It allows updating prior beliefs with new evidence.

**Q: What is the definition of a matrix determinant?**
A: The determinant of a square matrix is a scalar value that encodes information about the linear transformation it represents, including whether the matrix is invertible (non-zero determinant means invertible).

**Q: What is a prime factorization?**
A: Prime factorization is the unique decomposition of a composite integer into a product of prime numbers, guaranteed by the fundamental theorem of arithmetic.

**Q: What is the chain rule in calculus?**
A: The chain rule states that the derivative of a composite function f(g(x)) is f'(g(x)) · g'(x), allowing differentiation of nested functions.

**Q: What is the definition of a group in abstract algebra?**
A: A group is a set with a binary operation satisfying closure, associativity, existence of an identity element, and existence of inverse elements for every element.

**Q: What is Taylor series expansion?**
A: A Taylor series represents a function as an infinite sum of terms based on its derivatives at a point: f(x) = Σ f^(n)(a)/n! · (x−a)^n, enabling function approximation.

**Q: What is the Gaussian (normal) distribution?**
A: The Gaussian distribution is a continuous probability distribution with density f(x) = (1/σ√(2π)) e^(−(x−μ)²/(2σ²)), characterized by its mean μ and standard deviation σ.

**Q: What is a Fourier transform?**
A: The Fourier transform decomposes a function into its constituent frequencies: F(ω) = ∫ f(t) e^(−iωt) dt. It converts signals from the time domain to the frequency domain.

**Q: What is the concept of a limit at infinity?**
A: The limit of f(x) as x approaches infinity equals L means f(x) can be made arbitrarily close to L by taking x sufficiently large, describing asymptotic behavior.

**Q: What is Euler's identity?**
A: Euler's identity states e^(iπ) + 1 = 0, elegantly connecting the five most fundamental constants in mathematics: e, i, π, 1, and 0.

**Q: What is the concept of convergence in sequences?**
A: A sequence (a_n) converges to limit L if for every ε > 0, there exists N such that for all n > N, |a_n − L| < ε. Divergence means no such limit exists.
