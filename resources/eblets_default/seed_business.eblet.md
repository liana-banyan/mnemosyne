## Facts

**Q: What is a SWOT analysis?**
A: SWOT analysis is a strategic planning framework that identifies Strengths and Weaknesses (internal factors) and Opportunities and Threats (external factors) to guide decision-making.

**Q: What is supply chain management?**
A: Supply chain management coordinates the flow of goods, information, and finances from raw material suppliers through manufacturers and distributors to end consumers, optimizing for efficiency, cost, and resilience.

**Q: What is discounted cash flow (DCF) valuation?**
A: DCF values a business by discounting future cash flows to present value using a discount rate (typically WACC). The formula: NPV = Σ CF_t / (1+r)^t. A higher discount rate decreases present value.

**Q: What is Porter's Five Forces?**
A: Porter's Five Forces analyzes industry competitiveness: threat of new entrants, threat of substitutes, bargaining power of suppliers, bargaining power of buyers, and rivalry among existing competitors.

**Q: What is the difference between B2B and B2C business models?**
A: B2B (business-to-business) involves transactions between companies, often with longer sales cycles and larger deal values. B2C (business-to-consumer) involves selling directly to individuals, typically with shorter cycles and lower prices.

**Q: What is accounting's double-entry bookkeeping?**
A: Double-entry bookkeeping records every transaction as both a debit and a credit, maintaining the accounting equation (Assets = Liabilities + Equity). It ensures the books balance and provides audit trails.

**Q: What is organizational culture?**
A: Organizational culture is the shared values, beliefs, and norms that shape behavior within an organization. It influences employee motivation, decision-making, and organizational performance.

**Q: What is a business model canvas?**
A: The Business Model Canvas (Osterwalder) describes a business on nine blocks: customer segments, value propositions, channels, customer relationships, revenue streams, key resources, key activities, key partnerships, and cost structure.

**Q: What is customer lifetime value (CLV)?**
A: Customer Lifetime Value is the predicted total revenue a business will earn from a customer over their entire relationship. CLV = Average Purchase Value × Purchase Frequency × Customer Lifespan. Acquiring new customers costs 5-25x more than retaining existing ones.

**Q: What is the concept of economies of scale?**
A: Economies of scale occur when average production costs fall as output increases. Fixed costs spread over more units; specialization and bulk purchasing reduce variable costs. Diseconomies of scale reverse this at very large sizes.

**Q: What is the difference between leadership and management?**
A: Management focuses on planning, organizing, and controlling processes to achieve defined goals. Leadership focuses on inspiring, motivating, and guiding people toward a vision. Effective organizations need both.

**Q: What is net present value (NPV) in capital budgeting?**
A: NPV = present value of cash inflows − initial investment. Positive NPV indicates value creation; negative NPV destroys value. It is the primary criterion for evaluating investment projects in corporate finance.

**Q: What is market segmentation?**
A: Market segmentation divides a broad market into subsets of consumers with common needs or characteristics. Segments can be demographic, psychographic, geographic, or behavioral. Targeting allows more effective marketing.

**Q: What is the concept of competitive advantage?**
A: Competitive advantage is what allows a company to outperform competitors. Porter identified two types: cost leadership (producing at lower cost) and differentiation (unique attributes justifying premium prices).

**Q: What is franchise business model?**
A: A franchise grants a franchisee the right to operate under the franchisor's brand and systems in exchange for fees and royalties. It allows rapid expansion with lower capital risk than direct ownership.

**Q: What is financial leverage?**
A: Financial leverage uses debt to amplify returns on equity. While it can boost ROE when returns exceed debt costs, it amplifies losses when returns are poor. High leverage increases financial risk and bankruptcy probability.

**Q: What is the role of corporate social responsibility (CSR)?**
A: CSR refers to a company's voluntary commitments beyond legal requirements to benefit society: environmental sustainability, ethical labor practices, community investment, and transparent governance.

**Q: What is brand equity?**
A: Brand equity is the value premium that a company generates from a recognizable name compared to a generic equivalent. Strong brand equity enables premium pricing, customer loyalty, and lower marketing costs.

**Q: What is the concept of price discrimination?**
A: Price discrimination charges different customers different prices for the same product based on willingness to pay. First-degree: individual pricing. Second-degree: quantity-based. Third-degree: segment-based (student discounts).

**Q: What is the Pareto principle (80/20 rule) in business?**
A: The Pareto principle states that roughly 80% of effects come from 20% of causes. In business: 80% of revenue typically comes from 20% of customers; 80% of problems from 20% of defects.
