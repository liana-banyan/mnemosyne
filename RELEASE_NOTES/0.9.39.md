MnemosyneC 0.9.39: what is new

Share a card to a peer, on consent, with a signed receipt. One node offers one of its verified method cards to one other node. The receiver checks the signature, the card's source and its hash, then decides: your own machines land it automatically, a known peer's card waits in an inbox until you press accept, a stranger is refused and nothing is kept. The receiver signs a receipt saying what it did, and the sender verifies that receipt before believing it. Refusals get receipts too. The pull direction (copy a peer's shared library into yours) has been in the app since 0.9.31; this is the push. Tool: mnem_share_eblet_to_peer, mode push.

Publish a question from the app. Prove It, proof 2 ("Somebody Else Asked"): publish through publish_question with a persona picker and "Take it back", so a question you asked lands on mnemosynec.org/asked/ for anyone to read and hash-check, without the browser form.

The fourth consent. Settings now carry "accept offered cards from": your own machines (default), every enrolled peer, or named peers. Never strangers.

Carried forward from 0.9.38: the eight security fixes (the private signing key is no longer uploaded anywhere; exported proofs carry the signer's key; service-role branches removed from client code).

Known and open: the first published answer on /asked/ lost its line breaks (fix in progress); the Conduit's READ ME still says 0.1.3 inside the 0.1.5 zip; the cross-machine share has been proven between two processes on one machine, the two-house run is next.

Every answer reproducible, every miss published. Check my math: https://mnemosynec.org/prove/
